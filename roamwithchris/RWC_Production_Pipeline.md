# ROAMWITHCHRIS — PRODUCTION PIPELINE v1
**Start to finish, one reel. Owner per step. Use until ingrained.**
Strategy note: build the LONG master edit first when footage supports it; cut shorts FROM it. Shorts are trailers for the archive. Cross-post one 9:16 master to IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok (native uploads, no watermarks — platforms deprioritize watermarked reposts).

## STEP 1 — CAPTURE (during trip) — Chris
- Follow the Standing Capture Kit (companion doc).
- Within 48 hours: voice-memo the raw story telling. This seeds the Bible.

## STEP 2 — STORY TRIAGE — Chris (2 min)
- Kill Criterion: who sends this to whom, and why — one sentence or PARK.
- Cover concept: can you picture the frame-1 image and its 0–3 word text? No → concept is weak.

## STEP 3 — BIBLE (Core sections) — Chris + Claude
- Pull previous episode's carry-forward change FIRST (from last Retention Review).
- Complete Core: Hook, Turn (if real), Cast Check, Beats+Shots, Ladder, Lesson, Caption Block.
- Artie assist: match beats to clips from the footage catalog → output "beat 7 → GX015862, 0:14–0:19."
- Every beat resolves: HAVE / AI / CUT. Chris approves. No script before approval.

## STEP 4 — SCRIPT — Claude drafts, Chris owns
- Voiceover to the Bible. 45-sec max. 3–5 words per line.
- Chris reads it OUT LOUD once before locking — spoken rhythm ≠ written rhythm.
- Voiceover recorded via ElevenLabs clone (or live read if the story wants raw).

## STEP 5 — EDIT — Chris + tools (the bottleneck; automate hard)
Order of operations:
1. **Descript**: import clips per Artie's beat-to-clip map → text-based rough cut → one-click silence/filler removal. This is the single biggest time saver in the stack; use it before touching a timeline.
2. **Hook pass**: obsess over seconds 0–3 ONLY. Frame 1 = pattern interrupt + cover text (0–3 words, center-safe). Nothing else matters until this works.
3. **Cut pass**: every clip under 1 sec, zero dead air, beats connect by BUT/THEREFORE.
4. **CapCut**: word-by-word kinetic captions (85% watch muted), center 80% safe zone, min 36pt body / 48pt hook text, white-on-dark contrast.
5. **Music + audio wilds** last.

## STEP 6 — PRE-PUBLISH CHECK — Chris (3 min, as a stranger, ON MUTE)
- Does frame 1 stop a thumb at grid size?
- Does the hook's promise get paid off?
- Series handshake lands at ~sec 4–6, after the first beat?
- Captions readable, center-safe on all three platforms?

## STEP 7 — PUBLISH — Chris (Artie preps copy)
- Caption first line = second hook (all that shows before "...more").
- Pinned comment = lesson breakdown.
- 3–5 hashtags, filing labels only.
- IG: set cover (custom or strong frame — grid consistency builds follows from profile visits).
- YouTube Shorts: keyword title + custom thumbnail (Shorts ARE search-indexed; thumbnail shows on channel page/search, not feed).
- TikTok: set cover frame + 0–3 word cover text (profile grid is an underused follow-converter).
- Native upload on each platform. Same master file, per-platform captions.

## STEP 8 — RETENTION REVIEW — Chris + Claude (48–72 hrs post, 5 min HARD CAP)
- Fill Section 13 of the Bible: hook rate, hook type/subject log, drop-off timestamp, opener position, actual vs predicted triggers.
- Write the ONE carry-forward change. It is mandatory input to the next Bible's approval.

## CADENCE COMMITMENT
- Target: 2–3 reels/week sustained beats 5/week for two weeks then burnout. Consistency is the algorithm's favorite signal.
- **The rep counter: 20 reels shipped with completed Retention Reviews before judging the channel at all.** The KPI at this stage is reps-with-feedback, not followers. Storytelling skill is the asset; the channel is the gym.

## LONG-FORM RUNWAY (later)
- Stories with 15+ strong beats get flagged in Bible Extended field for a future YouTube long-form master (8–15 min per the Master Plan).
- Long-form master → cut 3–5 shorts from it → shorts drive discovery → channel page converts to long-form watch time. This is the Shorts→long funnel that YouTube uniquely rewards.

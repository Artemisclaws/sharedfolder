# Session S54 — 2026-07-01
**Topic:** roam

## The Problem
S53 ended with the Story Protocol locked and the Auggie backpacking reel Story Bible built, but the actual script unwritten. The plan was simple: confirm the Bible, write the script in one clean pass, done. It didn't stay simple — the Bible was referenced as living in "memory item #7," and that memory doesn't exist in this environment. The real problem this session opened with wasn't the script. It was whether the system we built last session could survive a session boundary at all.

## Questions We Were Trying to Solve
- Is the Auggie reel Story Bible actually accessible, or was it only ever in Chris's head and ChatGPT's context window?
- Can the final script get written in one clean pass, the way the protocol promises?
- Is the Story Bible template itself good enough, or does outside feedback (ChatGPT) expose real gaps?
- What's actually stopping Chris from editing faster — is it the editing, or is it that he can't find the right footage?
- Can AI already do prompt-driven editing, or is that still years out?
- Where should terabytes of footage from five different sources even live?
- Can Artie help with any of this right now?
- Is a footage archive organized by trip enough, or does it need to be organized by story?
- How much structure is too much structure for a 10-15 minute per-trip index?

## What We Tried That Didn't Work
- **Assuming the Story Bible was loaded.** It wasn't — "memory item #7" is a Claude.ai persistent-memory reference that doesn't carry into this Cowork session. Caught it before writing anything, asked Chris directly instead of fabricating a Bible from the SPRINT.md summary.
- **Taking ChatGPT's Story Bible critique at face value.** It proposed 10 additions. Roughly half were genuinely valuable (Compass, false-belief line, the Auggie-inheritance section); the rest either duplicated what was already locked in Voice/POV or added friction disproportionate to a 45-second format. Adopted the good half, explained why the rest got cut.
- **Taking ChatGPT's expanded Story Index schema at face value, twice.** The second pass ballooned to roughly 30 fields while still claiming a 10-15 minute fill time — a direct contradiction, and the same overload failure already logged in the S53b journal. Trimmed to 20 fields across 6 groups, kept the genuinely new leverage (permanent Story ID, Theme as first-class, Story Rating), cut the duplicated production metadata that Resolve already captures automatically.
- **Defaulting to "yes, Artie can do this."** Checked EMPIRE_STATUS instead of assuming — Artie is explicitly flagged as unreliable and not a Phase 1 dependency. Said so plainly instead of overpromising.

## What We Built
The Auggie backpacking reel script — 37 numbered cuts, written in one clean pass off a complete Bible, with every Must-Never-Forget beat intact. This was the protocol's first real test since being written down, and it passed: no rewrites, no forgotten characters, no third-person slips.

Beyond the script, the session turned into something bigger — a full content architecture for RoamWithChris. The Story Bible Protocol got a real second draft (v2): a Compass section naming why a story deserves to exist and what image nobody forgets, a false-belief line feeding the lesson, and a new section asking why Auggie specifically needs this story — the piece that turns a travel reel into an inheritance. Above the Bible, a new artifact got designed: the Story Index, a fast per-trip catalog that acts as the searchable menu of every story sitting on Chris's drives, with the Story Bible reserved for whichever trips actually get selected to become episodes.

Separately, real research got done on the actual bottleneck Chris named himself: not editing taste, but finding footage across external drives, microSDs, and three different camera sources. Semantic search tools already do this well. Full autonomous editing doesn't exist yet and won't for a while — the emotional judgment calls stay human. A three-phase, budget-conscious plan got staged: consolidate onto the one external HD Chris already owns, tag for free in DaVinci Resolve, upgrade to paid AI tools only once volume justifies the cost.

## Key Decisions
- Story Bible Protocol locked at v2 — provisional bedrock, proven on one story shape, not yet stress-tested against a structurally different trip
- Story Index and Story Bible are separate artifacts at different weights — menu vs. recipe — not one bloated document
- Theme promoted to a first-class Story Index field, capped at 1 primary + 2 secondary — the highest-leverage structural call of the session, since the audience follows themes, not trips
- External HD stays the source of truth over Google Photos, which becomes a real cost once video volume passes the free tier
- Wideframe and NAS are correct answers for later, not now — priced and sized for a studio, not a solo creator still practicing
- Artie stays out of the footage pipeline until reliability is proven — no automation gets built on an unproven foundation

# Session S53b — 2026-07-01
**Topic:** roamwithchris — Story Protocol + Auggie Backpacking Reel Story Bible

## The Problem
RoamWithChris had no repeatable system for producing story-driven reels. Every time a new video was discussed, the script got rewritten from scratch — forgetting characters, POV rules, tone, structure, and key story beats. The Auggie backpacking reel went through multiple rewrites because there was no single locked reference. Chris kept having to correct the same things: wrong POV (third person instead of "you"), missing characters, wrong structure, no lesson format, no series signature.

## Questions We Were Trying to Solve
- What is the right storytelling structure for this type of content?
- Who are the storytelling comps and what do they do well?
- What makes a reel go viral — and how does the 12k reel formula apply here?
- What should the series signature opener be?
- How do the stories link across trips and years to feel like one life story?
- Where do lessons live — in the voiceover, the comments, or both?
- What is the hook for this specific reel?
- How do we make sure we never forget these elements again?

## What We Tried That Didn't Work
- **Writing the script before building the foundation** — went through at least 6 rewrites because characters, POV, beats, and structure weren't locked first. Chris caught it: "Every time we rewrite you forgot something."
- **Third-person references to Auggie** — repeatedly written as "Auggie did X" instead of "you did X." Corrected multiple times.
- **Pumpkin as a first-timer** — initially written as Pumpkin's first trip. Corrected: Pumpkin is a 15-year-old veteran who stayed home by choice this trip, carried by Golfii on trail.
- **Havasupai framing** — initially written as if it was just Chris and Golfii. Corrected: Kate was there too, Auggie was in the belly, Pumpkin stayed home.
- **"Once upon a time" as the signature opener** — too generic. Replaced with a ChatGPT-assisted process that produced 10 options.
- **Timestamp signature** ("Summer 2026") — rejected because it doesn't age well when stories are told out of chronological order.
- **2–4 second cuts** — too slow. Corrected to under 1 second per cut, which changes the entire script format to short punchy fragments.
- **Rating mechanic** ("7 out of 10") — borrowed from the 12k reel format but doesn't fit the bedtime story format. Dropped.

## What We Built

### Story Protocol — Permanent Operating Procedure
Before any script, reel, or narrative content for RoamWithChris: build the Story Bible first. No script until Bible is complete and Chris approves it. Bible must include: Voice/POV, Characters, What/Where/When/Why, Every story beat in order, Goals, Narrative methods, Technical requirements, Must Never Be Forgotten list. Locked in memory and SPRINT.

### Story Bible — Auggie Backpacking Reel (complete, ready for script)
All elements locked: five characters with roles and moments, every story beat from 6am departure to Loch Leven, hook format, cut pacing, caption style, music, export specs, lesson line, save triggers, pinned comment format, Instagram caption, YouTube title and description.

### Series Signature Opener
"You won't remember this. So let me tell you." — works for any story, any time, any place, forever. Selected via ChatGPT prompt from 10 options. Option 1 was the winner.

### Series Tagline
"This is one of the stories I kept for you." — for Instagram bio and YouTube channel description.

### Lesson Format
One line woven into voiceover near the end + full lesson breakdown in pinned comment + future compilation reel of lessons across trips. This links the stories across time and gives viewers a reason to save and return.

### Series Architecture
The brand reframe: Chris isn't a travel influencer. He's a father leaving his son a handbook for life. Every hard thing done, every mistake made, every lesson learned — it goes in the book. For Auggie. Each reel is a chapter. Chapters don't need to be in order. At the end of it all — when Auggie is old enough to watch — it's one complete life.

## Key Decisions
- **Story Protocol is the law** — no script without a complete approved Bible
- **Always "you" never "Auggie"** — Chris speaks to Auggie directly, always
- **Series signature:** "You won't remember this. So let me tell you."
- **Series tagline:** "This is one of the stories I kept for you."
- **Cuts under 1 second** — script lines must be 3–5 words max each
- **Lesson in every reel** — one line voiceover + pinned comment
- **Save triggers at the end** — location + gear, natural not forced
- **Hook format locked** — Auggie hero visual → AI baby text → signature opener

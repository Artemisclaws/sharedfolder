# Session S52 — 2026-06-24
**Topic:** aura-sweet

## The Problem
Aura Sweet had four ice cream flavors in three different sizes at inconsistent prices. No customer avatar. No competitive intelligence. No market data. A real product with real early sales and no foundation underneath it.

## Questions We Were Trying to Solve
- What size should the ice cream be? (3.5 oz vs 5 oz vs 7 oz — all active, none consistent)
- Who is actually buying this?
- How do we reach them?
- Which marketing frameworks actually apply to a tiny delivery-native Thai dessert brand?
- Is TikTok safe for a small business owner who hasn't downloaded it yet?

## What We Tried That Didn't Work
- Nothing failed. This was a research and foundation session. The only near-miss was starting without GitHub loaded — caught immediately and corrected.

## What We Built
The foundation Aura Sweet was missing. Started with a sizing question and ended with a complete market intelligence file that will outlive this session and serve every future business Chris launches on Atlantic Ave.

The customer avatar came into focus fast once Chris shared the real data: 100 Thai tea sales from a poster, 35 fish sauce caramels, 427 mango sticky rice orders on DoorDash in two months. These aren't projections. These are real buyers who showed up without being invited. The avatar wrote itself from the evidence.

Bixby Knolls turned out to be a better market than the business plan gave it credit for. Median income $103,777 — 19% above the Long Beach city median. Median age 40. Families. A Thunderbolt Pizza line 10 feet away and a Ramen Hub full of dessert-hungry kids down the street. First Fridays bringing thousands to Atlantic Ave every month.

The competitive picture landed at the end: Somisomi and Ding Tea sit next to Ramen Hub. Somisomi is the direct competitor — a chain, Instagram-native, popular with kids, fish-shaped taiyaki cones. The insight is that Somisomi's chain nature is the weakness. Aura Sweet has what Somisomi cannot manufacture: a real Thai kitchen, real flavors, and a story that is true.

## Key Decisions
- Sizing standardized: 8oz cup and 16oz pint. Every flavor, same two sizes, consistent pricing. The 3.5/5/7oz fragmentation is over.
- Marketing frameworks: Hormozi (offer architecture — make the pint a no-brainer), Godin (fish sauce caramel is the Purple Cow, lead with it), Cialdini (anchor on the pint, engineer first reviews).
- Instagram Reels is the content channel. TikTok deferred — real privacy concern, real regulatory uncertainty, Reels reaches the same demographic anyway.
- BIXBY_KNOLLS_MARKET.md created as a permanent shared asset. Any future Pinyo business on Atlantic Ave loads this file first.
- Aura Sweet product scope confirmed: ice cream + gelato + broader Thai dessert formats (Kanomwann-style), not just ice cream cups.

## What We Built

The Finance tab is live at ops.radrooster.co. After weeks of data sitting in XLS files and Google Sheets, the numbers are finally visible in one place: a YoY comparison chart, three hero metrics, a monthly detail table, and five action recommendations. This was not a small build — it required parsing UTF-16LE XLS files via base64, reconstructing Q1 monthly splits from ratios, doing apples-to-apples May comparisons, and wiring it into the existing dashboard without breaking the Ops or Revenue tabs.

We also upgraded CLAUDE-CORE.md to V5. Three real protocol improvements came directly from observed pain: test logic before building, no re-scanning files already loaded, use Haiku agents for mechanical work. These came from Chris calling out inefficiency directly. That kind of direct feedback makes the system better.

## The Numbers That Matter

- **YTD 2026 vs 2025: -7.5%**. This is not noise. Every single month in 2026 is below the same month in 2025.
- **April was the worst: -11.3%**. No known cause yet.
- **May is tracking -7.6%** (apples-to-apples, 26 days).
- **BOH labor: $494/day**. Fixed cost. Runs whether the restaurant is full or empty.

The revenue decline is real and documented. But the more important unknown is COGS. Without knowing what food costs as a percent of revenue, we cannot know whether Aura Thai is structurally profitable at current volume. That is the next session.

## What Was Hard

The session ran long. Context limits hit. Scripts got stuck. The base64/UTF-16LE parsing was tedious. Chris had to prompt multiple times to get back on track. The handoff itself nearly got abandoned.

None of that is acceptable as a recurring pattern. The protocol changes in V5 are the response.

## The Focus Filter

Chris said: "Goal right now is to keep our doors open. At this rate we are not sustainable."

That sentence sets the priority for everything that follows. The sequential path is:
1. **COGS tracking** — know what food actually costs
2. **Fix delivery pipeline** — stop flying blind on 3PD revenue
3. **Unit economics** — revenue − COGS − labor − overhead = what is actually left
4. **Then grow**

New ideas go to the parking lot. The $5K/day filter is active.

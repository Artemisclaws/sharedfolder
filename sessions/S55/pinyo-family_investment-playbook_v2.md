## 4 · GOLFII — ROTH IRA (age 32)

### The wall you need to know about first
**Married Filing Separately + living together = Roth contributions phase out at $0–$10K MAGI.** If her MAGI is over $10K, direct Roth contributions are **blocked**. Also under MFS there is **no spousal contribution** — her cap is the lesser of **$7,500 (2026 limit)** or **her own W-2 earned income**. And in California (community property), her MFS MAGI may not equal her W-2 — income gets split between returns. This is exactly the tax-strategy conversation to have.

### The legal path that works regardless of income: Backdoor Roth
1. Open **Traditional IRA + Roth IRA** in Golfii's name (Fidelity, same login ecosystem).
2. Contribute to the Traditional IRA as **nondeductible** (cap: lesser of $7,500 or her W-2 income).
3. Convert to Roth shortly after. No income limit on conversions.
4. File **Form 8606** with her return — non-negotiable paperwork.

**Pre-check (blocking):** she must have **no existing pre-tax IRA balances** (Traditional/SEP/SIMPLE/old 401k rolled to IRA), or the pro-rata rule taxes the conversion. Confirm before step 2.

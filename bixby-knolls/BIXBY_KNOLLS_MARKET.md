## FOOT TRAFFIC SOURCES

### 1. Thunderbolt Pizza - HIGHEST PRIORITY
- **Proximity:** 10 feet from 4085 Atlantic Ave
- **Volume:** Lines out the door, extending in front of Aura Thai
- **Customer:** Families, 30s-50s, already spending $40-60 on pizza
- **Opportunity:** Captive audience waiting in line = zero-cost impulse window
- **Tactic:** A-frame sign facing their line. One message. One price.

### 2. Ramen Hub - HIGH PRIORITY
- **Proximity:** Down the street on Atlantic Ave
- **Volume:** Always at capacity
- **Customer:** Families with kids, food-adventurous, actively seeking dessert after meal
- **Adjacent businesses:** Dessert place + tea place next door (direct competitors for post-ramen spend)
- **Opportunity:** Customers leaving Ramen Hub are the most qualified walk-in dessert leads on the block
- **Tactic:** Directional signage, social content targeting the "after ramen" moment

### 3. First Fridays - Atlantic Ave (Monthly, no July)
- **What it is:** Monthly community event - thousands walk Atlantic Ave, businesses open late, live music, vendors
- **Schedule:** Monthly except July
- **Next opportunity:** August 2026
- **Customer:** Full neighborhood cross-section - families, couples, locals, visitors
- **Opportunity:** Highest single-night foot traffic on the block
- **Tactic:** Pop-up station at business entrance. Create content at the event.
